Assumptions

stockcue runs advisory math on the data you provide: average daily demand, demand variability, your lead times and review cadence. It does not know about seasonality, promotions, supplier minimums, or your cash flow. It is decision support, not financial or purchasing advice. All parsing and calculation happens locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
